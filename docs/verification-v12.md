## Limits

Circuit scores describe declared toy-world outcomes. Race, stunt and map values
are fictional. Creative scores check pitch, cues, synchronization, choreography
envelopes and production operations; human artistry remains a separate review.
Official research uses frozen synthetic fixtures. Template commentary reads
masked public replay data and cannot award points or mutate the world.

Teams are editable operational identities with retained evidence, not verified
people or sentient entities. Existing local-model support is optional and requires
an explicit connection; no real Ollama qualification was run. Presentation buses
are interface contracts without external traffic, logins, publishing or services.
Native archive size and device quota remain finite; exports provide explicit
operator retention. Batch variance is descriptive and team outcomes within a
match are coupled; no confidence or general-intelligence claim is made.
