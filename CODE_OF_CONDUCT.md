# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and players with respect. Welcome different backgrounds, ages, abilities, and communication styles. Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.

Harassment, threats, sexual content directed at minors, discrimination, doxxing, and disclosure of private information are not acceptable. Do not request private contact details or personal information from young players or contributors.

Give constructive feedback with clear examples. Keep public discussions focused on the project. If a conversation becomes unsafe, disengage and report it through an appropriate private platform reporting channel.

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation to keep the project safe. Community participation does not require agreeing with every design choice; respectful disagreement is welcome.
