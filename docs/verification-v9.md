## Boundaries and implemented limits

Receipts referenced as artifact/memory provenance cannot be silently discarded. Export before adding a dependent run that cannot fit. Profiles retain their existing 3 MB import boundary. Recorded holdout/transfer notes and artifacts cannot become automatic career context.

The V7 career adapters accept fresh inputs only; their original independent episode notebooks and optional explicit local-model connection remain in the Garage. World outcomes can include team actions and are not attributed as universal individual skills.
